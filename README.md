# awesome-resume-cn

> A curated list of **free** resume / CV / job-hunting resources for Chinese speakers.
> No paywalls, no account required. Just useful stuff.

## 🛠 Builders & Templates
- [简历大师 Resume Master](https://markmiller1.github.io/resume-master/) — 免费、纯前端、隐私优先的简历生成器，64 套模板 + 面试/谈薪指南，数据不出本机
- [牛客简历](https://www.nowcoder.com/resume) — 校招简历模板与范例
- [超级简历 WonderCV](https://www.wondercv.com/) — 在线简历编辑器（部分高级功能收费）
- [五百丁](https://www.wordoc.com/) — 简历模板社区

## 📚 Guides & Examples
- [简历范文（知页）](https://www.zhiye.com/) — 各行业简历范文
- [应届生求职网](https://www.yingjiesheng.com/) — 应届生简历与招聘
- [Offer 帮](https://www.offerbang.io/) — 求职面试资料

## 🤖 AI Assistants
- [ResumeWorded](https://www.resumeworded.com/) — AI 简历评分与优化建议（英文为主）
- [ResumeAI](https://withresumeai.com/) — Free ATS checker (3/day anonymous, 10/day free account); State of ATS 2026 (Workday 37.9%)
- [Rezi](https://www.rezi.ai/) — AI 简历生成器

## 📖 Reading
- [简历撰写指北](https://github.com/resumejob/awesome-resume) — 中文简历编写指南合集
- [dyweb/awesome-resume-for-chinese](https://github.com/dyweb/awesome-resume-for-chinese) — 为中文用户整理的简历资源
